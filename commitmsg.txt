Sprint 7: admin configuration back office, plus six bugs found while building it

Admin area (was a placeholder, now /admin and 34 more routes):
  - Dashboard: today's queue, plus what needs a human decision (services with no
    bookable slots, clients holding a booking on a declared holiday, realtime
    health) and one-click slot regeneration.
  - CRUD for service windows, services, slot templates, and holidays.
  - Day-by-day slot capacity with open/close and bulk rescale.
  - Staff and administrator account management.
  - Filterable audit trail.

Rules live in ScheduleAdminService and StaffAdminService rather than the
controllers, so they cannot be skipped by adding another entry point.

The foreign keys are deliberately not relied on. appointments.service_id is
restrictOnDelete, so deleting a booked service raised a raw integrity error - a
500 with a stack trace for what is really "deactivate it instead". And
services.service_window_id is cascadeOnDelete, so deleting a window would have
silently deleted every service in it. Refusals now raise
AdminActionBlockedException with a message stating the blocker and a hint stating
the way out.

Guards: a window with services or staff cannot be deleted; a service with any
appointment or any still-bookable slot cannot be deleted; slot capacity cannot
drop below booked_count (below that the slot is permanently over-subscribed and
the clients already booked vanish from it); a template whose slot length exceeds
its opening window is refused (the generator would emit nothing, leaving the
service silently unavailable); the last active administrator cannot be demoted,
deactivated, deleted, or have their password self-reset.

Moving a service to another window now also moves its time_slots and its UPCOMING
appointments - those rows are what the console and lobby display group by, so a
service left behind kept appearing on the old window's board. Completed
appointments are left alone: they record what actually happened.

Refused actions are audited too (service.delete_blocked, user.change_blocked,
...). A trail showing only successes reads as an all-clear.

Bugs found and fixed along the way:

- nextAvailableSlot() ignored the booking horizon, so with a 30-day horizon the
  service page advertised a slot 40 days out - a date the booking screen then
  refused to open - while a free slot three days away went unmentioned.
- openSlotCount() and nextAvailableSlot() ignored declared holidays, so the
  catalogue could claim availability on a day the office was shut. All three
  answers to "can a client book this?" now share one bookableQuery().
- SlotGeneratorService clamped capacity with max(existing, template), so the old
  number was always larger and lowering a template from 4 to 2 changed nothing on
  any slot ever generated. The floor is booked_count, which is what the comment
  always claimed.
- Duplicate service/window codes 500'd on the UNIQUE constraint instead of
  showing a validation message (same bug already fixed for holidays).
- No navigation at all below 768px: the primary links were hidden md:flex with
  no mobile replacement, so anyone on a phone could only reach Home, Services, or
  the Queue Display by typing the URL. Now a native <details> disclosure.
- An administrator had no link to the staff console, because the nav used
  @elseif and the admin branch came first - even though admins may open any
  window's console.
- Duplicate element ids on the slot-template page: one form per template all
  sharing field ids, so clicking a label focused the wrong control and
  aria-describedby announced the wrong help text.
- old() is global, not per-form, so a rejected save refilled every other form on
  the page with that rejected input.
- Four <th> with no scope on the client's appointment history.
- Two different font weight lists between layouts: the app layout requested
  400-800 while the views use font-black (900), so every font-black heading
  silently rendered at 800.

479 tests passing (was 381). Pint clean.
